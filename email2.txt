Notice of Infringement traintimes.org.uk

Dear Internet Service Provider,

Transport for London (TFL) owns some of the most famous trademarks in the transport industry, including the LONDON UNDERGROUND, UNDERGROUND, the ROUNDEL, TRANSPORT FOR LONDON, LONDON BUSES and OYSTER trademarks. These trademarks are used to identify, advertise, and promote Transport for London’s products and activities.  They are protected around the world under local and international trademarks.  

Transport for London markets and sells its products and services through websites including https://tfl.gov.uk/.  Transport for London’s trademarks are registered in many countries worldwide and include: EU Registration Nos. 11196532 and 11961554 for the London Underground mark, 299206 for the Roundel mark, and 299578 for the Underground mark.  

You are the Internet Service Provider to traintimes.org.uk .  This site uses Transport for London’s trademark(s) in its content without authorization.  This use falsely suggests Transport for London’s sponsorship or endorsement of the website and violates Transport for London’s exclusive rights.

Additionally, this use constitutes trademark infringement and unfair competition under the Lanham Act; and it violates the Anti-Cybersquatting Consumer Protection Act, 15 U.S.C. section 1125.  Finally, it violates your acceptable use policy as an Internet Service Provider.

We informed the registrant of our complaint, but were unable to resolve this issue.  In addition to the legal action Transport for London may take against the registrant, we request that you immediately suspend the site from publication for the offenses outlined above.
